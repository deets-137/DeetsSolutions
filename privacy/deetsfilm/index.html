Privacy — DeetsFilm

DeetsFilm is a browser extension that notices when the film you are watching is ending and offers you a link to its Letterboxd page. The short version: it stores nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and has no account.

What DeetsFilm collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixel, and no server belonging to this project that the extension talks to.

What it does on your computer

  • On the streaming sites you enable, it reads how far through the video you are and the title on the page. That is the whole of what it looks at. It cannot see the picture, and it does not read your account, your profile, your viewing history, or anything else on the page.
  • It keeps your settings — whether the card appears automatically, whether it shows poster art, how early it appears, and which services it watches — in your browser's extension storage. Those never leave your device.

The one request it makes

To turn a title into the right Letterboxd page, DeetsFilm asks The Movie Database to look the film up. That request contains the film's title and nothing else — no identifier, no account, nothing about you. TMDB is a third party with its own privacy policy, and like any request your browser makes, it carries your IP address to them.

Results are cached on your device so re-watching a film asks nobody anything.

Letterboxd

DeetsFilm never signs in to Letterboxd, never logs a film for you, and never touches your account. Clicking the card opens a normal Letterboxd page in a normal tab; everything after that is between you and Letterboxd, under their terms and their privacy policy. DeetsFilm is not affiliated with Letterboxd or with any streaming service.

Permissions, and why

Your browser will say DeetsFilm can read and change data on the streaming sites you turn on. It needs that to see the video's progress and the film title on the page. It asks for each service only when you enable that service, and for nothing else — turning a service off withdraws it.

Changes

If DeetsFilm ever starts sending anything anywhere, this page is updated before that ships, not after. Any such feature would be off by default and would ask first.
