Features

  • Last.fm Scrobbling

    [Had to be the first one listed yk] Live logging your listening history to Last.Fm so you can make your own rewinds and get better recs.

  • Discord Status + Webhooks

    Optionally have what you're listening to live update as a Discord status. Can also add a Disc channel to post Song of the Days to.

  • Airplay

    Link up w any airplay speaker and stream just DeetsMusic audio or your entire PC audio (figured it'd be cool but not by default).

  • Mini, Midi and Max

    Different UI layouts for different sized app size. Mini and midi don't take a lot of space and hold a panel or two if that's all you need.

  • 6 themes × 4 skins

    24 looks matching the website (for now lol). Themes are color schemes on top of the skins' skeleton (unique animations, style, etc).

  • Equalizer

    Tune the bass, treble, and more with a 10 band EQ. Set your own presets (please and let me know any changes you want there lol).

Only in DeetsMusic

  • Listening rooms

    Add friends in app & start a room where you and your friends can listen to music, queue up songs, and vibe together.

  • MCP & CLI Interfaces [For Nerds]

    An MCP and CLI allow your preferred AI system to make a playlist, queue up songs, and operate the app for you. Disabled by default.

Known issues

    Privacy

    Apple sign in is purely on your PC, DeetsMusic can't see anything about your Apple account.

    There's no cloud backups or anything yet so no one except Apple gets your play history.

    When your app or this page checks a report you sent, the time is noted, so Aditya knows his reply reached you. No ID, no IP address.
