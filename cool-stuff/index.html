DeetsBeats

built

A Bluetooth watchdog that keeps my headphones in stereo.

Windows loves to yank a headset out of A2DP the moment any app touches a mic. DeetsBeats sits in the tray, watches the connection, suppresses the Hands-Free profile until a mic is actually wanted, pins the right audio endpoint, and climbs a reconnect ladder when the link drops — same Tauri-and-Rust stack as DeetsMusic for Windows.

Tauri v2 Rust Win32 Bluetooth

DeetsJack

building

A blackjack trainer disguised as a blackjack game.

Play full hands, then flip the trainer card to see the live math — dealer bust odds, your bust odds, and the EV of every move. It scores decision quality, not luck: the +EV move is “right” even when the hand loses. The pure-Swift EV engine is unit-tested against published basic-strategy numbers.

Games

DeetsCities

live

A hex-board trading game, playable in a browser tab.

Share a table code and go — no install, no account. Every table is its own Durable Object holding the whole game, and each player gets a personalized view because hands and dev cards are hidden. Drop out mid-game and your seat waits 30 seconds before a bot takes over, then hands it back when you return — and the host can seat bots outright, each with its own difficulty tier. The worker behind it lives in its own private repo.

Cloudflare Workers Durable Objects no framework

DeetsMahjong

live

Four-seat Hong Kong mahjong, same table, same rules.

Built on the shared table foundation DeetsCities runs on, with the hidden information taken seriously — your hand, your freshly drawn tile, and your own claim options never leave your connection. The rules engine is a pure module that checks itself — the bots live inside it too, in three difficulty tiers — and tiles come in two decks you pick for yourself. Its worker is a separate private repo.

Cloudflare Workers Durable Objects rules engine

DeetsPoker

live

No-limit hold’em cash tables with friends.

The third game on the shared table foundation. Money is integer cents and every bet has to break into real chips; hole cards ride only your own connection, and even the bots can’t peek — a bot sees exactly what a player in its seat would. Step away and you choose what leaving costs: your stack waits, banks, or forfeits. Its worker is another private repo.

Cloudflare Workers Durable Objects integer cents

DeetsShips

building

Team-vs-team naval warfare, captains and all.

The first real-teams game on the table foundation: two sides of 1–4 seats, one captain per side committing the team’s plan each round. Everything hidden is hidden per TEAM — ship positions, staged plans, and sonar results never cross the line; the enemy sees only intel marks, while spectators see everything. The worker is deployed and the tab is in its final pass before it joins the nav.
